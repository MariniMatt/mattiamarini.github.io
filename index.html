Steps to a TPI score

The problem

The resume gap

A resume reflects past experience, but reveals little about a candidate's actual performance or future potential.

Interview inconsistency

Interviews remain subjective and inconsistent across interviewers, and candidates rarely receive useful feedback.

The solution

TPI

TalentOS simulates real interview scenarios through artificial intelligence and returns a Talent Performance Index — a standardized score that objectively measures candidate performance, useful to both companies and candidates themselves.

How it works

  1. 01
    AI-guided simulationCandidates go through an interview that replicates real work scenarios.
  2. 02
    Language & speech analysisAn advanced NLP model evaluates communication, behavior, and soft skills.
  3. 03
    TPI score generationCollected data is converted into a standardized performance index.
  4. 04
    Personalized feedbackCandidates receive practical guidance and tools to improve.

The market

Core HR software53%
Talent assessment & analytics28%
AI-based screening19%

With over 80 million applications sent every year and no universal evaluation standard, the opportunity for TalentOS is large and still largely uncontested.

Business model

B2C — individual

Freemium model with free basic access; premium subscriptions unlock advanced simulations, detailed analytics, and personalized coaching.

B2B — enterprise

SaaS subscriptions for unlimited assessments, or per-assessment pricing: scalable solutions for teams of any size.

Competitive positioning

Existing platforms screen candidates but offer no growth tools or feedback. TalentOS is the only player combining high evaluation standardization with high candidate development.

"To become the global standard for measuring talent potential — just as GPA standardizes academic performance, TalentOS standardizes talent assessment."

— the TalentOS vision

FY2025 results

Ferretti Group

Net revenue from new yachts of €1,232 million (+5.0%), adjusted EBITDA of €202.8 million (16.5% margin), a net cash position of €111 million, and an order backlog of €1.72 billion.

Sanlorenzo

Net revenue from new yachts of €960.4 million (+3.2%), EBITDA of €180.6 million (18.8% margin), net profit of €107.4 million, and an order backlog approaching €2 billion.

Ferretti generates more revenue thanks to the group's scale — seven brands spanning composite boats to superyachts — but it is Sanlorenzo that delivers the higher margin, supported by its semi-custom model and the integration of Nautor Swan.

Reading the results

FY25

Ferretti leans on brand diversification and volume to defend its market leadership; Sanlorenzo stays focused on fewer, higher-value units, translating that choice into a higher EBITDA margin. The comparison illustrates two legitimate paths to growth within the same industry: scale versus selectivity.
